Digital Banking Account Opening System :

Business Requirements:
Login: Customer should be able to login using: - Username - Password 

Registration: Customer should provide: - National ID - Full Name - Mobile Number - Email 

OTP Verification: - OTP should be generated - OTP should expire after 2 minutes 

Account Opening: Customer can create account if: - Age >= 18 - Saudi National ID - National ID not registered before 

Dashboard: Customer can view: - Account Number - Account Status - Customer Information
